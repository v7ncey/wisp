Use it on ChatGPT and Claude

The Chrome extension runs the same coach on chatgpt.com and claude.ai. It reads only the text box and your sent messages on those two sites, and keeps its memory on your computer.

  1. Download the extension (.zip) and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.

Not on the Chrome Web Store yet. AI personalization is off until you turn it on from Wisp's menu.
